Job Title: Human Resources Business Partner / Manager
Department: People Operations
Location: Remote / Hybrid

Role Summary:
We are seeking an experienced Human Resources professional to manage full-lifecycle people operations, talent acquisition, and workforce planning. You will partner with business unit leaders to drive employee engagement, performance management, and organizational development.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead end-to-end talent acquisition, recruitment, interview coordination, and employee onboarding processes.
- Administer performance management cycles, career pathing, compensation management, and promotions.
- Resolve employee relations matters, provide mediation and conflict resolution, and ensure compliance with employment and labor law.
- Oversee payroll processing, benefits administration, and company wellness programs.
- Coordinate corporate training and development initiatives using modern learning management systems (LMS).
- Analyze HR analytics and workforce metrics to optimize retention, headcount planning, and diversity and inclusion (D&I) goals.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- 3+ years of progressive HR experience in recruitment, people operations, or as an HR generalist.
- Hands-on experience with modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and HR information systems.
- Thorough knowledge of labor law, employment compliance, and standard workplace policies.
- Demonstrated success in conflict resolution, organizational change management, and employee relations.
- Strong written and verbal interpersonal communication skills with discretion regarding confidential data.
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, or related discipline.
