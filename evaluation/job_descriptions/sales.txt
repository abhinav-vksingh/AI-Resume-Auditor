Job Title: Senior Enterprise Sales / Account Executive
Department: Commercial Sales
Location: Remote / Field

Role Summary:
We are looking for an ambitious Senior Sales Executive to lead B2B software and service sales, manage enterprise accounts, and drive revenue growth. You will manage the entire sales pipeline from prospecting and product demonstrations through commercial negotiation and deal closing.

Key Responsibilities:
- Generate new business opportunities through outbound prospecting, cold calling, and inbound lead qualification.
- Manage full-cycle B2B sales cycles with senior decision-makers, navigating procurement, legal, and security reviews.
- Conduct tailored, compelling product demonstrations to illustrate ROI and strategic value for prospective clients.
- Maintain accurate sales forecasting, deal stages, and customer activity logs in CRM software (Salesforce).
- Partner with customer success teams on account management, client retention, contract renewals, and upselling / cross-selling.
- Consistently achieve or exceed quarterly and annual quota attainment targets.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- 3+ years of successful track record in B2B direct sales, enterprise business development, or account management.
- Demonstrated mastery of CRM software (Salesforce, HubSpot) and pipeline management methodologies.
- Proven skill in strategic selling, consultative qualification, objection handling, and commercial negotiation.
- Exceptional oral presentation, verbal articulation, and relationship-building capabilities.
- Self-motivated, target-driven mindset with disciplined time and territory management skills.
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, or equivalent sales experience.
